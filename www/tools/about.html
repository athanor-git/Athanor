Your data & privacy

Everything you make — saved charts, the Daily Journal, card scans, your edits to card meanings, quiz progress and settings — stays on this device. The app sends nothing anywhere and has no accounts, tracking or ads.

Your location (for sunrise, planetary hours, Resh and the compass) is used on the device only.

Sources
  • Liber 777 — Aleister Crowley (1909): the correspondence tables.
  • The Book of the Goetia of Solomon the King — S. L. MacGregor Mathers & Aleister Crowley (1904): the 72 spirits.
  • The Lesser Key of Solomon — L. W. de Laurence (1916): the seals.
  • Book T — The Equinox I:8 (1912): the divinatory meanings and the elemental dignities.
  • Liber Resh vel Helios, Liber XV, Liber Samekh, Liber V vel Reguli, the Star Ruby — Aleister Crowley.
  • The Heptameron (tr. Robert Turner, 1655), Three Books of Occult Philosophy — H. C. Agrippa, Theurgia-Goetia (Lemegeton), Pseudomonarchia daemonum — J. Weyer: the compass attributions.
  • Golden Dawn papers (as published by Israel Regardie): the temple quarters.
  • The planetary dignities follow the Thoth Tarot – Planetary Dignities reference sheet.
  • The short card summaries are original wording, written after Lon Milo DuQuette's Understanding Aleister Crowley's Thoth Tarot (2003). The pathworking journeys are original.
  • Sun, Moon and planets are computed on the device (JPL Keplerian elements, Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms).

Tarot pictures are never included: the card scanner photographs your own deck.
