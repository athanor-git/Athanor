Scan cards

The scanner finds every card in the photo, straightens it like a document-scanner app and evens out the lighting. You check the corners before anything is saved.

Tips for a good photo
  • Plain, contrasting background — a dark cloth or table under light-bordered cards (or a light one under dark cards). Busy patterns confuse the scanner.
  • The whole card in the frame, with a little space around every card.
  • Cards not touching — leave a finger’s width between them. Several cards in one photo is fine.
  • Avoid glare — overhead lamps reflect off glossy cards. Tilt the phone a little, or use daylight from a window.
  • Hold the phone roughly parallel to the table; a slight angle is corrected automatically.
  • Fill the frame. A scan is only as detailed as the card is in the photo — for the sharpest scans, photograph one or two cards at a time, close up.

Check the corners

Drag the round handles onto each card’s corners — a magnifier shows what is under your finger. Drag inside an outline to move it; tap an outline to select it (for “Remove”), and “Zoom in” to work on a small card. A dashed outline is one the scanner is unsure of.
